Study - Deterministic Roulette
Objective
Create a 3D single-player Unity prototype of a Deterministic Roulette game, 
focusing on immersive roulette wheel animations, robust data tracking, and 
standard Roulette rules. The game should track gameplay and winning statistics. 
“Deterministicˮ indicates that the player can manually choose which number will 
appear on the next spin, enabling testing or controlled gameplay scenarios.
Features
1. Roulette Wheel & Animations
Deterministic Outcome Selection The player can select the next winning 
number through a UI element before spinning. If not selected, the game can 
produce a random number.
Wheel Spinning Animation A 3D roulette wheel that spins smoothly, showing 
a realistic ball drop animation.
Visual Cues Clear indication of winning numbers and their colors 
(red/black/green).
2. Player Statistics
Win / Loss Tracking Track the number of spins played, total wins, and overall 
profit/loss.
Historical Record Display these statistics in the UI or on a dedicated 
screen/menu.
3. Full Roulette Rules & Mechanics
Betting & Chip System Required component where players can select 
different chip denominations and place bets on the roulette table.
